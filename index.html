Help and support

Classic Movies Quiz is a film-history quiz for iPhone and Android, about films up to 1999, in English and Spanish. It works offline.

Getting Premium back

Premium is a one-time purchase. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle all payments, so they also handle refunds.

  • iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: your Google Play order history

Your progress

Your answers, scores and bookmarks are saved only on your phone. To start over, open Profile › Settings › Reset local practice. Deleting the app also deletes your progress, and it can't be brought back.

Language

Switch between English and Spanish in Profile › Settings › Language. Your progress stays the same.

Leaderboards

Ranked rounds send your score to Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android. Sign in to that service on your phone to take part. Leaderboards are optional; the rest of the quiz works without them.

Contact

Found a wrong answer, a bug or a typo? Email jimkang12345@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model and, for a question, a few words of it.

Privacy

The app keeps your data on your phone and has no ads or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
