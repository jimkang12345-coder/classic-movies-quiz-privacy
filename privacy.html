Privacy policy

Classic Movies Quiz for iPhone and Android

Last updated 13 October 2026

The short version

  • The quiz works offline. Your answers, progress, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone and are never sent to me.
  • I don't run any servers for the app. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you don't need an account.
  • Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. I never see your card or payment details.
  • If you use the leaderboards, your ranked scores go to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games.
  • I don't sell or share your personal information.

Who is responsible

Classic Movies Quiz is made by Jimin Kang, an independent developer based in South Korea (“I” or “me” in this policy). I am responsible, as the data controller, for the small amount of personal information described below.

Questions or requests: jimkang12345@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these on your device only:

  • your answers, round history, scores, levels and streaks;
  • your bookmarks;
  • your settings, such as language, sound, music, motion and question preferences;
  • a note that Premium is unlocked, so Premium also works offline;
  • ranked scores waiting to be sent to a leaderboard.

None of this is sent to me. On Android, the app keeps it out of cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers. On iPhone, your progress is kept out of backups; your settings may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, which Apple handles.

To erase your practice history, open Profile › Settings › Reset local practice. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Buying Premium

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google takes the payment and handles your payment details under their own privacy policies. The app only learns whether you own Premium (on Android, together with a purchase token that confirms the purchase with Google) and saves that on your phone. “Restore purchase” simply asks Apple or Google again.

Apple and Google send me sales reports so that I can be paid and keep accounts. These show details of each sale, such as the date, price and country, and sometimes the buyer's region or postal code. They do not show your name or payment details. I use them for accounting, tax, refunds and preventing fraud, because the law and our sales agreement require it.

Leaderboards (optional)

If you sign in to Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android, the app sends your best round score and your total points from ranked rounds to that service, linked to your game profile. Apple or Google shows the leaderboards to other players according to your profile settings. If you allow it, the service also shows your friends' scores; the app does not keep your friends list.

Apple and Google process this under their own privacy policies, and Google's Play Games software may also collect technical and diagnostic information for Google's own purposes. I can see leaderboard entries (player name and score) and remove scores that break the rules. You use leaderboards only if you choose to. To stop, sign out of Game Center or Play Games, or don't play ranked rounds. To remove your scores, use your Game Center or Play Games settings, or ask me.

More information: Game Center and privacy, Google privacy policy.

Links to other websites

Answers link to their sources, such as Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Those pages open in your web browser, and their own privacy policies apply there.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you open them, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details, as any web server does, and keeps them for a short time for security, under the GitHub privacy statement. The pages set no cookies and load nothing from other websites, and I receive no information about visitors.

When you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and what you write, and I use it only to answer you. My email provider, Google (Gmail), stores these messages for me. I delete support emails within two years of our last message, unless I need them longer to deal with a legal claim. I rely on my legitimate interest in answering you.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting service of my own.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No accounts, sign-up or login.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, as those terms are used in California law.

Online features, such as a daily challenge against other players, are not part of this version. If I add them, I will update this policy first, and they will be optional.

Children

Classic Movies Quiz is a general-audience film quiz and is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in South Korea). It never asks for anyone's name, age or contact details. Apple's and Google's family settings control what children can buy. If you think a child has emailed me personal information, tell me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me what personal information I hold about you and ask me to correct or delete it. Because the app keeps your data on your phone, in practice I only hold support emails and sales reports. I will answer within one month, and it costs you nothing.

In the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to restrict or object to how I use your information, to receive it in a portable form, and to complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

In California and other US states with privacy laws, you have the right to know, delete and correct personal information. I don't sell or share personal information, I don't use sensitive personal information, and I won't treat you differently for using your rights.

The same requests are open to you under other laws, such as Brazil's LGPD and Canada's PIPEDA. Write to jimkang12345@gmail.com.

Where information is handled

I am based in South Korea. Apple and Google run their stores and game services worldwide and move information between countries under their own safeguards. Support emails are stored by Google, which may keep them in the United States and other countries under its own safeguards. I don't transfer your information anywhere else.

Security

Your quiz data stays on your phone, protected by your phone's own security, such as its passcode and encryption. The app uses the internet only for purchases, leaderboards and links you choose to open.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top. If a change affects what information the app handles, I will also say so in the app's update notes before the change takes effect.
